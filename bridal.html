Good to know

Booking terms, in plain words

No surprises. Here is how advance, changes and at-venue service work.

30%advance locks your date
15 daysfree date change before the wedding
25 kmat-venue included in Royal
How do I lock my date?

A 30% advance locks your date and your artist. The balance is paid on the wedding morning, before we begin.

Can I change my wedding date?

Yes, once and free of charge, up to 15 days before the wedding. It depends on availability for the new date.

What if I cancel?

If you cancel 30 or more days before the wedding, the advance is refunded less a ₹1,000 booking fee. Within 30 days, the advance is non-refundable.

How does at-venue service work?

Our artists come to your home, hotel or banquet. Royal covers up to 25 km from the studio and Maharani up to 40 km. Beyond that, a travel charge applies. Early muhurat calls before 6 AM have a small early-call fee.

Is the trial really included?

Every package includes a trial. Extra trials are ₹1,999 each. Bring photos of your outfit and jewellery so we can plan the look.
