Questions we hear a lot

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but an appointment gets you your preferred artist and no waiting. Weekends fill up fast, so book ahead if you can.

Do you do a patch test before colour or skin treatments?

Yes. We do a quick, free patch test before colour and for sensitive-skin treatments. Please tell us about any allergies when you book.

How early should I book bridal makeup?

Wedding dates fill quickly, so book 2 to 3 months ahead. A trial session usually happens 3 to 4 weeks before the wedding. Details are on our bridal page.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 4 hours before your slot. Bridal bookings have separate terms, which we share when you enquire.

Which products and tools do you use?

Professional salon brands, and single-use strips and tools for waxing and facials. If you prefer to bring your own product, just tell us.
