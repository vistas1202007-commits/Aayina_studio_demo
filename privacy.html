A little clarity

Your details, thoughtfully handled.

When you explore the demo

The demo stores favourites, the cart and sample appointments in your browser. No OTP or appointment is sent. Use “Reset demo data” in the app to remove its saved demo data. Photos are AI-generated, and names and reviews are fictional.

When the studio enables live booking

Verified sign-in uses your name and mobile number. Your appointments, enquiry details and optional profile preferences are saved on the studio server. Appointment notes are visible to authorised studio staff; avoid adding sensitive information that is not needed for your visit.

Messages and third-party services

Phone verification uses the configured SMS provider. Appointment confirmations and opted-in reminders may be sent through the configured notification provider. The WhatsApp buttons open a message draft; you choose whether to send it. Browser favourites and the service cart stay on your device.

Access and corrections

You can view your appointments, edit profile details, sign out, and change reminder preferences in the app. To request removal of server records or ask about retention, contact the studio through its published contact details. The operator must publish its actual contact, retention and provider details before accepting real customer data.

Offline access

The app caches public pages and images for offline browsing. Authentication, personal API responses and the studio dashboard are not cached by the service worker. Booking changes require an internet connection.
